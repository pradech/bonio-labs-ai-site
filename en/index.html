Frequently asked questions

Which organizations is this for?

Organizations where at least one team already uses AI and that want to know where to invest next. If nobody in your organization uses AI yet, start with basic training first.

What does our team need to prepare?

One project sponsor, 6-8 interviewees for 45 minutes each, work volume figures for the past 3 months, and sample data with personal data removed.

How will our data be used with AI?

Your data enters only the AI tools you approve in writing before work starts, and testing uses only sample data with personal data removed.

How much does it cost?

A fixed price per business unit. We send a quotation after the first call, and you know the full price before work starts.

Does the price include building the system?

No. This service covers choosing the work AI should take on and proving its value. System development and AI tool costs are separate.

What happens after the 10 days?

Your executives decide whether to run the pilot. You can run the plan yourselves, or have Bonio Labs support the pilot.
