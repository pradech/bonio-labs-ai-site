Examples of tasks that often make the list

Problems large enterprises run into often. Each dot in the picture above is a task like these. In every case people still review and decide. These are general examples, not results from any client.

  1. 01Operations

    Too many documents to review in time

    Problem
    Large volumes of applications or supporting documents have to be read and summarized one set at a time.
    How AI helps
    Reads the documents and drafts a summary in the team's own template for staff to check and correct.
    Measured by
    Time per document set, and the share of summaries that need edits
    This sounds like us

  2. 02Customer service

    Customers ask the same questions every day

    Problem
    The team spends most of its time on questions the handbook already answers.
    How AI helps
    Drafts replies from the handbook and past answers for an agent to approve before sending.
    Measured by
    First response time, and cases closed per person
    This sounds like us

  3. 03All departments

    Nobody can find internal information

    Problem
    Policies, manuals and meeting decisions are scattered, so staff keep asking the same experts.
    How AI helps
    Answers questions from internal documents with the source cited, within the asker's access rights.
    Measured by
    Time to find an answer, and questions escalated to experts
    This sounds like us

  4. 04Finance and planning

    Recurring reports eat hours every week

    Problem
    Figures are pulled from several systems and the commentary is rewritten by hand each cycle.
    How AI helps
    Combines the data, drafts the report and flags unusual figures for the owner to check.
    Measured by
    Hours per reporting cycle, and errors found afterwards
    This sounds like us

  5. 05Executives and project teams

    Meetings end and nothing moves

    Problem
    Decisions and follow-ups stay in personal notes and never reach the work tracker.
    How AI helps
    Turns the meeting into tasks with owners and due dates, and records them in the work system.
    Measured by
    Share of meeting actions with an owner, and actions done on time
    This sounds like us

  6. 06Compliance and legal

    Documents are checked against rules by hand

    Problem
    Contracts or filings are compared with requirements line by line, which is slow and easy to miss.
    How AI helps
    Compares the document with the requirement list and flags what needs attention for an expert to judge.
    Measured by
    Review time per document, and issues caught before approval
    This sounds like us

Frequently asked questions

        

          

            Which organizations is this for?
            
Large enterprises with several business units, where at least one team already uses AI and executives want evidence before investing further. If nobody in your organization uses AI yet, start with basic training first.

          

          

            What does our team need to prepare?
            
One executive sponsor, 6-8 interviewees for 45 minutes each, work volume figures for the past 3 months, and sample data with personal data removed.

          

          

            How do you work with our IT, information security and compliance teams?
            
We invite all three from day one, use only the AI tools your organization approves in writing, and test only on sample data with personal data removed.

          

          

            How much does it cost?
            
A fixed price per business unit. We send a quotation after the first discussion, and your organization knows the full price before work starts.

          

          

            Does the price include building the system?
            
No. This service covers choosing the work AI should take on and proving its value. System development and AI tool costs are separate.

          

          

            What happens after the 10 days?
            
Your executive committee decides whether to run the pilot. Your organization can run the plan itself, or have Bonio Labs support the pilot and repeat the same method in other business units.

Privacy policy

        
Bonio Labs collects the email you enter, and anything you choose to add such as the service you are interested in, your organization size and a message, only to contact you about its services. We do not sell your data or pass it to third parties for marketing.

        
When you send a form, we also record where your visit came from, such as the campaign link you followed. If you send a result from the calculator, we receive the numbers you entered as well.

        
Form data is sent through the form service provider Web3Forms to the Bonio Labs email inbox. You can ask us to delete or correct your data by replying to the email our team sends you.
