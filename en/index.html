Frequently asked questions

        

          

            Which organizations is this for?
            
Large enterprises where a team already uses AI and executives want evidence before investing further. If nobody uses AI yet, start with training.

          

          

            What does our team need to prepare?
            
One executive sponsor, 6-8 interviewees for 45 minutes each, work volume figures for the past 3 months, and sample data with personal data removed.

          

          

            How do you work with our IT, information security and compliance teams?
            
All three join from day one. We use only the AI tools your organization approves in writing, and test on sample data with personal data removed.

          

          

            How much does it cost?
            
A fixed price per business unit. We send a quotation after the first discussion, before work starts.

          

          

            Does the price include building the system?
            
No. This service covers choosing the work AI should take on and proving its value. System development and AI tool costs are separate.

          

          

            What happens after the 10 days?
            
Your executive committee decides whether to run the pilot. You can run it yourselves, or have Bonio Labs support it and extend it to other business units.
